# My Favorite Movie: How to Train Your Dragon (2010)

> *"This is Berk. It's twelve days north of Hopeless, and a few degrees south of Freezing to Death. It's located solidly on the Meridian of Misery."* — Hiccup Horrendous Haddock III

---

## About the Movie
* **Directors:** Chris Sanders and Dean DeBlois
* **Release Year:** 2010
* **Studio:** DreamWorks Animation
* **Genre:** Animated, Fantasy, Action, Adventure
* **Setting:** The Viking island of Berk

## Main Characters & Voice Cast
* **Hiccup** (*voiced by Jay Baruchel*) — A creative, misfit Viking teenager who befriends an injured dragon.
* **Toothless** — A rare, intelligent Night Fury dragon who loses part of his tail fin.
* **Stoick the Vast** (*voiced by Gerard Butler*) — The mighty chief of Berk and Hiccup's father.
* **Astrid Hofferson** (*voiced by America Ferrera*) — A fierce, competitive young Viking warrior.
* **Gobber the Belch** (*voiced by Craig Ferguson*) — The village blacksmith and Hiccup's mentor.

## Trilogy Overview
| Movie | Release Year | Key Highlight |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **How to Train Your Dragon** | 2010 | Hiccup and Toothless form an unbreakable bond and bring peace between Vikings and dragons. |
| **How to Train Your Dragon 2** | 2014 | Hiccup reunites with his mother, Valka, and defends Berk from Drago Bludvist. |
| **How to Train Your Dragon: The Hidden World** | 2019 | Toothless discovers a hidden dragon utopia and Hiccup makes the ultimate sacrifice to protect them. |

## Why I Love It
1. **Heartwarming Friendship:** The wordless trust-building scene (*"Forbidden Friendship"*) between Hiccup and Toothless is one of the most iconic moments in animation history.
2. **Breathtaking Score & Visuals:** John Powell's soundtrack—especially *"Test Drive"*—pairs brilliantly with the exhilarating flying sequences.
3. **Meaningful Message:** It teaches empathy, courage, and challenging outdated traditions through understanding rather than fear.
